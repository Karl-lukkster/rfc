On The Radar
These options are strong, maybe problematically so.
BN1's Anubis
BN1 has low damage compared to the rest of the series. Anubis is one of few options that isn't reduced by armor. There are only a few chips that can remove Anubis, making it difficult to remove in a singleton format.
BN3's VarSwrd
Effectively 800 damage, VarSwrd is one of the strongest chips in the series. It's more "execution" than "interactive". However, there is skill in the execution. If VarSwrd required no inputs, it'd be overwhelming.
BN4's Dark Soul
Dark soul has two benifits: access to dark chips, and Dark Soul Unison - inviciblity and uncontrollable actions when they would be deleted. Dark soul has a couple of downsides as well: no access to "light" chips, and permanent max HP drops. Dark soul is both strong, and works against the "one save" convenience of HZ Star. As once dark soul is obtained, the save is forever worse as a light soul save.
BN6's BgDthThd
BgDthThd turns MegaMan's buster into a thunder ball. This leads to players purposefully not using chips, favoring buster wars and slow gauge. We want HZ Star to exhibit unique folder decisions, not use one chip and buster away.
BN6's BugRSwrd
BugRSwrd faces the same issues as BgDthThd, centralises folder building and replaces the need for chips. However, BugRSwrd is noticeably worse. BugRSwrd has range issues, and doesn't linger in the same style as BgDthThd.
Star Force
We haven't put much thought into Star Force yet. We think it'd work. Star Force doesn't have the same problems Battle Network has, so it might not be as needed. Star Force also has noticeably less folder restrictions, as Cards don't have lettered codes the same way they do in Battle Network. It would soft-ban Bushido folders though.
What's a "Star Chip" in Star Force?
Any Mega class Card. However, Mega class cards obtained through a brother-card shuffle are allowed to then be used. HZ Star is a rule set on what one can include in their folder, not how one can play the game.
How do we manage Brother Bands with No Online rules?
All out-of-game Brother Bands are banned. We're concerned this'll make Mega Man Rogue and Rogue noise stronger than intended.
Star Force Legacy Collection?
It'd definitely make doing Star Force HZ Star easier.
