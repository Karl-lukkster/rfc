Rogue's Folder Challenge
Custom format designed for Mega Man Battle Network and Mega Man Star Force. Build a folder of 30 unique chips, without having access to Navi Summons.
High level rules:
Singleton format, meaning only one of each chip by name allowed in ones folder.
Navi Summons, that is Star Chips () in BN, and Mega Cards in SF, are not allowed.
All online exclusives are banned.
